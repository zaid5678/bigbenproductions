Based in the UK, Bigben Production is a full-service creative studio specialising in photography, videography, and experiential event technology. With years of experience across weddings, birthdays, podcasts, and corporate events, we combine technical precision with genuine storytelling instinct.

From our immersive 360° video booths to our custom LED display installations, every tool in our arsenal exists to make your event unforgettable — and to give your guests something to talk about long after the night ends.

We work closely with every client from initial enquiry through to final delivery, ensuring your vision is understood and realised exactly as you imagined it.
