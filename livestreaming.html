What We Provide

Broadcast Your
Moment Live.

Whether loved ones can't make it in person or you want to extend your reach beyond the venue, our professional live streaming service ensures nobody misses a thing. We handle every technical detail — you stay present in the moment.

From single-camera intimate ceremonies to multi-camera productions with graphics overlays, we tailor every stream to your event and audience.

Multi-platform simultaneous streaming — broadcast to YouTube, Facebook, and custom RTMP endpoints at the same time

HD & 4K quality — crystal-clear video so remote viewers feel like they're in the room

Professional audio capture — dedicated microphone setup, not just camera audio

Custom graphics & lower thirds — branded overlays, name titles, and on-screen elements

Full recording included — receive a complete archive of the stream after the event

Dedicated on-site operator — a technician manages the stream throughout so nothing is left to chance

How It Works

Simple Setup,
Flawless Broadcast.

01

Enquiry & Brief

Tell us about your event — date, venue, platform preference, and expected viewer numbers. We'll recommend the right setup.

02

Venue Pre-Check

We assess internet connectivity and acoustics ahead of time to ensure a stable, high-quality stream on the day.

03

On-Site Setup

Our team arrives early to position cameras, run audio, configure the encoder, and run a full test stream before guests arrive.

04

Live Broadcast

We go live and monitor the stream throughout — adjusting cameras, audio levels, and graphics in real time.

05

Recording Delivery

A full high-resolution recording is delivered to you after the event, ready to share or archive.
